Department of Computer Science

The Department of Computer Science at Pondicherry University provides students with a strong foundation in computer science, programming, software development, and emerging technologies.

The department focuses on academic excellence, practical learning, research, and the development of technical and problem-solving skills required for the modern technology industry.

Infrastructure

The department provides well-equipped computer laboratories, classrooms and learning spaces that support practical education, programming and technical activities.

Facilities

Students have access to computer systems, internet facilities, digital learning resources, laboratories and other academic facilities to support their studies.

Academic Excellence

The department encourages students to strengthen their programming skills, participate in projects, explore emerging technologies and develop innovative ideas.
